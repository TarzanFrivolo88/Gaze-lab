Webcam eye tracker

Estimates where on the screen you're looking, tracks which line you're reading, and logs simple focus signals. Everything runs on this computer.

  • Light your face from the front. A window behind you makes tracking much worse.
  • Sit at arm's length (about 50–70 cm), with the camera at the top of the screen.
  • Keep your head fairly still after calibrating. If you move a lot, press D (drift fix) or recalibrate.
  • Glasses are fine, but strong reflections on the lenses hurt accuracy.
  • On a phone: prop it up rather than holding it, about 25–35 cm from your face. Pick portrait or landscape before calibrating, and recalibrate if you rotate it.

Calibration

Look straight at each dot until it shrinks and moves on. Follow it with your eyes, and try to keep your head still.
